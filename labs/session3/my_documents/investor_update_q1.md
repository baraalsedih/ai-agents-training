# Khazeen — Monthly Investor Update (Q1)

## Summary

Six weeks since our last update. Pilot cohort is now at seven restaurants,
and we've made a firm call on geographic expansion plus made progress on
the pricing question that was still open last time.

## Decision: expanding to Sahel first

After the advisory board review, we decided Sahel will be our first
expansion city after the home market, ahead of the other candidate cities
we scouted. Restaurant density there is higher and it's close enough that
our current small sales team can support it without new hires yet.

## Evidence: the inventory loss number keeps holding up

Across every independent conversation we've had with restaurant owners —
including our newest pilot customer — the estimate for monthly losses from
poor inventory tracking keeps landing in the same $300–350 range. This is
now the strongest, most consistently repeated data point in our pitch.

## Idea: SMS alerts, not just in-app notifications

A pilot customer suggested stock-out alerts should also go out over SMS,
not only as an in-app notification, since owners don't have the dashboard
open during rush hour. We like this but haven't costed it yet.

## Open question: subscription pricing vs. per-item pricing

We still haven't fully closed the debate between a flat monthly
subscription and pricing based on the number of items a restaurant
manages. Early willingness-to-pay signals are encouraging either way, but
we want another month of trial data before locking the model in.

## Ask

No fundraising ask this round — this is a progress update only. Next
update will include the Sahel launch numbers.
